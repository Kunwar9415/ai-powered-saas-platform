Artificial intelligence and deep learning are transformative disciplines within computer science. A neural network consists of layers of interconnected nodes or artificial neurons. The transformer architecture relies on multi-head self-attention mechanisms to process sequence data in parallel. Unlike recurrent neural networks, transformers do not suffer from sequential processing bottlenecks. In a causal decoder-only transformer, attention masks ensure that token predictions depend exclusively on preceding tokens. Positional encodings provide the necessary spatial information regarding token positions in the input sequence. Optimization algorithms such as Adam and AdamW adjust model weights iteratively using backpropagation of gradients.
